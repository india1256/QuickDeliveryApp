How Quick Delivery Works

  • 1. Transparent Booking & Fare Seal: Enter pickup & drop locations, choose Bike, Scooter, Auto, or Mini-Truck (up to 20 kg), and lock an upfront fare with GST invoice support.
  • 2. Pre-Pickup Photo & GPS Verification: Capture parcel safety photos before dispatch, track the assigned verified Rider on live map telemetry, and share the 4-digit Pickup OTP only at physical handover.
  • 3. Verified Delivery Proof & Digital Receipt: Delivery closes only after 4-digit Drop OTP / digital recipient signature and proof capture, generating an official Quick Delivery PDF Tax Invoice.

Parcel Safety, Tampering, Theft & Accident Protection

  • Zero-Tolerance Tamper & Theft Policy: Opening, replacing, stealing, or mishandling a sealed parcel results in immediate Rider account suspension, Aadhaar/DL/RC KYC blacklisting, payout hold, and legal police FIR cooperation with full GPS & photo evidence.
  • Road Accident or Transit Damage: Partners must report incidents immediately with live photos via In-App Support or support@quickdelivery.co.in. Verified claims are escalated to Admin Operations for settlement & resolution within 24–48 hours.
