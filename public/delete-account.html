Account & Data Deletion

Applies to: Quick Delivery, Quick Delivery Partner, and Quick Delivery Admin (com.aistudio.quickdelivery.*)

Option 1: Instant In-App Deletion

  1. Open Quick Delivery (Customer) or Quick Delivery Partner (Rider).
  2. Open the top-left Menu (☰) drawer.
  3. Tap Delete Account & Data and confirm.
  4. All local profile information, authentication tokens, and session backups are permanently erased immediately.

Option 2: Web & Server Data Deletion Request

To request permanent erasure of any cloud order logs or partner records stored on our dispatch servers, email ravindergupta141@gmail.com from your registered email address with your 10-digit mobile number. Verified requests are processed within 7 business days.
