Privacy Policy — Quick Delivery Suite

Official Website: https://quickdelivery.co.in • Policy URL: https://quickdelivery.co.in/privacy-policy
Apps Covered: Quick Delivery (Customer), Partner Quick Delivery (Rider), and Quick Delivery Admin
Developer / Data Controller: Quick Delivery Logistics (support@quickdelivery.co.in)
Effective Date: November 2026

1. Account-Isolated Information We Collect

  • Account & Partner Identity: Name, 10-digit mobile number, Google email address, and (for Riders) KYC documents strictly isolated per account for booking history, GST receipts, and payouts.
  • Foreground Location (Required Post-Login): Approximate and precise GPS coordinates collected only while booking, actively navigating, or tracking an active order. No background spying occurs when offline.
  • Camera & Media Storage (Required Post-Login): Parcel pickup/drop proof photos and Rider selfie check-ins captured when triggered by you, plus encrypted local backups of your profile, saved addresses, and order history stored safely in Phone Storage (Documents/QuickDelivery).
  • Notifications (Required Post-Login): Push and local tray alerts strictly for order status updates, OTPs, and live Rider-Customer chat.

2. How We Use & Protect Data

  • Used solely to calculate transparent delivery fares, verify OTP & digital signature handovers, display live rider ETA, generate GST/Earning PDFs, and verify online payments.
  • All traffic in transit is encrypted over HTTPS (TLS) with Firebase Zero-Trust rules and HMAC-SHA256 integrity verification.
  • We never sell personal data or share it with third-party advertising networks.

3. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account and profile anytime inside the app via Menu → Delete Account & Data, review web deletion instructions at https://quickdelivery.co.in/delete-account, or email support@quickdelivery.co.in.
